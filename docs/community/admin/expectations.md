# VS14 admin expectations

> **Note on the multi-admin language below.** Many sections here
> assume a multi-admin team. Today there's just one (the maintainer);
> rules that say "another admin," "second opinion," or `#admin-only`
> collapse to "the maintainer decides" until the team grows. We're
> keeping the language forward-looking so the doc is right when the
> team is right.

## The short version

Admins exist to keep the server a place people want to come back to.
That's the whole job.

## The role

VS14 admins are **volunteer moderators** with in-game and Discord
authority. You are not a developer, a GM, or a final-word arbiter on
lore. Think of it as being a bouncer at a small bar with a house rule
against punching — most of the job is noticing, chatting, and
occasionally asking someone to leave.

### Admins DO

- Respond to `ahelp` (F1 in-game) reports from players
- Enforce the server rules (`/rules`) consistently
- Issue warnings, kicks, temp bans, and permanent bans per the
  [sanctions](./sanctions.md) matrix
- Investigate griefing, harassment, and exploit reports
- Answer new-player questions when no mentor is available
- Log notable incidents using the
  [incident template](./incident-template.md)
- Hand off to another admin when you're tired, conflicted, or not
  sure — "I'm not sure, let me loop in someone else" is always the
  right call

### Admins DON'T

- **Don't play god.** No spawning items for friends, no self-assigning
  antag roles, no resolving a plot you dislike by fiat. The spectate
  + admin-verb surface exists to investigate, not to author.
- **Don't favor friends.** If a close friend breaks a rule, recuse
  yourself and hand the ticket to another admin. This is a
  zero-exceptions rule.
- **Don't retaliate.** A player insulting you in Discord does not
  earn a game ban. Personal friction goes to a different admin, full
  stop.
- **Don't release private data.** Ahelp content, IP addresses, hub
  account identifiers, incident logs — none of it leaves the admin
  channel without maintainer approval and a documented reason.
- **Don't permaban alone.** Permanent bans and hub-level (Wizden
  identity) bans require a second admin or the maintainer to sign
  off. See [sanctions.md](./sanctions.md).
- **Don't improvise on policy.** If the rule isn't clear, say so in
  `#admin-only` and get a ruling. Written precedent beats fresh
  judgment under pressure.

## Escalation ladder

Work small first. Escalate only when the next tier actually adds
information or authority.

1. **You handle it.** Warning, short kick, short temp ban — whatever
   is proportionate.
2. **Second opinion.** Ping another admin in `#admin-only`. Most
   ambiguous calls resolve here.
3. **Public communication.** Maintainer-only — anything reaching
   outside the admin team (Discord announcement, GitHub issue
   response, hub-moderator communication) goes through the
   maintainer.

## Privacy

Admin tools give you access to IP addresses, hub identifiers, Discord
account links, ahelp contents, and ban history. Treat all of it as
confidential — share with other admins or the maintainer when an
investigation needs it, never outside the team.

Specifically:

- Don't screenshot or paste ahelp content outside `#admin-only`
- Don't share a player's hub ID without a documented reason
- If external officials contact you about player data, route to the
  maintainer immediately — don't respond directly

## Conduct standards

Admins are visible representatives of VS14, on duty or off. How you
conduct yourself in public reflects on the project, so we expect a
baseline of professionalism and decorum:

- Civil tone in every public interaction, even with players who are
  being uncivil to you.
- Admin status does not buy you exemptions from the server rules.
  An admin griefing gets the same sanction a player griefing would.

Beyond those two, we trust admins to handle their own opinions and
participation in the broader SS14 community. We're not going to
police what positions you take or what you say in your own spaces.
