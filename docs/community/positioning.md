# VS14 positioning

The server's identity in words. Two versions, one source of truth.

- **Short** — inherits to the hub blurb, server browser tag, MOTD
  compression, Discord `#welcome` summary, and the website hero.
- **Long** — inherits to the about page, the launch announcement,
  and any longer-form Discord or website intro.

When this file changes, the downstream copy gets updated too.

## Short

Vacation Station 14 is a casual SS14 hangout server. Light roleplay,
creativity over violence, and a chosen-family vibe where players
share the space and take care of it together. A unique fork curated
from many sibling stations, with events that bridge Discord and the
game. SFW, 13+, hub-listed via the Wizards' Den.

## Long

Vacation Station 14 is a casual Space Station 14 server — a low-RP
hangout where the round is supposed to be fun. It's built for
players who don't have time to grind heavier-RP servers and just
want a regular community to drop into. Light roleplay sets the
tone: bring your character, make it work, but the bar is
participation, not pristine performance.

VS14 is a unique experimental fork. The base is pure SS14 with
curated cherry-picks from sibling stations woven in for content
variety, so the round doesn't match any other server you've played.
The through-line is creativity and collaboration over competition
and combat — what players build, scheme, and improvise together.
Community events bridge our Discord and the game, so the hangout
extends past any single round.

The community is casual and chosen-family — closer to a co-op than
a crew. We share the space, take care of it together, and trust
players to bring the bit. Roleplay can go in any direction; the
only hard line is no NSFW content. SFW server, 13+ welcome,
Wizards' Den hub-listed.
